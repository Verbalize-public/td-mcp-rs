# Verify the claim and hand back the work

Current-tool procedure. Verification follows the user's outcome, not a universal ceremony.

| Claim | Minimum relevant evidence |
|---|---|
| Parameter/wire/structure changed | Actual canonical target, selected value/mode or connector readback; affected-node diagnostic availability |
| Network is clean | Covered target set; each relevant node present; errors/warnings actually observed and available—not missing fields or swallowed reads |
| Shader compiled | Affirmative supported compiler evidence on affected consumers, with unknown/unsupported/truncated states kept distinct |
| Appearance matches | A viewed image of the claimed surface at stated conditions; code and a filename alone do not count |
| Motion/reset works | Controlled initialization/reset and sequential advancement; actual samples/offsets and stateful behavior checked |
| Sustained performance target met | Relevant live workload over wall time with conditions recorded—not target rate, stepped frames or movie metadata |
| Artifact saved/reusable | Actual authorized saved path/identity plus the reload/portability checks required for that deliverable |

## After a change

Verify a bounded set of changed canonical nodes, affected consumers/destinations and relevant parents. Do not claim recursive coverage from one parent inspect. Preserve per-field availability and missing-name lists. Inspect tool-level success and per-item state separately.

On partial mutation, preceding steps remain applied and fields in the failed step may have changed. Reconcile before repair. Verify deletions through the surviving parent's sufficient roster coverage and affected connections. An empty out-of-range page or one missing-node response is not sufficient proof of deletion.

Do not “fix” a test by suppressing the warning, editing a status field to PASS or weakening its acceptance. A failed diagnostic may be separate from the user's requested result; state the distinction. Pre-existing unrelated issues do not authorize repairing the entire project.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **Verified:** the relevant observed evidence supports this scoped claim.
- **Failed:** relevant evidence contradicts the requested behavior.
- **Unverified:** the check was unavailable or not run; say what is missing.
- **Not requested:** outside this task's acceptance, not a hidden failure.

Use only the dimensions relevant to the task in the final reply. A scalar edit may need one concise confirmation; a reusable animated asset needs more than a parameter readback. Test counts, comments and worker summaries are not independent proof of the actual artifact.

## Handoff

Name the delivered result and its location/identity, the important evidence, remaining scope and limits, and any material final process/transport state. Distinguish live unsaved changes, a saved project, a temporary capture job and a durable downloaded artifact.

Persist the minimum continuation facts when context/ownership changes: target identity, allowed scope, current canonical paths, saved/evidence locations, exact unresolved failures, completed versus unverified checks, and the next safe action. A retained plan is not a fresh scene snapshot or new permission. On resume, revalidate the facts the next consequential step depends on—not the entire manual or every previous probe.

If blocked, finish independent authorized work and give a precise receipt using [`recover`](./recover.md). Do not present reduced scope as full completion, and do not manufacture a blocker from uncertainty alone.
